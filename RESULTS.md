# CBIO033 results (PREREG.md and src/run033.py committed together at 244766b before any model was scored). Raw: results/results033.json, results/run033.log.
Data: GEO GSE137140, 1,566 pre-op lung cancer (LC) vs 2,178 non-cancer controls, 2,565 serum miRNAs; 180 post-op LC samples held aside. Everything below is 5-fold CV, seed 0, RF 300 trees; feature selection and fold-change bins computed inside each training fold only (sample sd ddof=1 is in the json; sds are all <= 0.01 here).
## What the abstract's pattern looks like on this cohort (D2, leakage-free)
Accuracy: HD (high dysregulation) 0.997, MD (medium) 0.994, ND (non-significant) 0.955; AUROC 1.000 / 0.9999 / 0.994. Prereg gates (HD >= 0.95, MD >= 0.90, ND >= 0.85) all met. The pattern "MD works almost as well as HD" is reproduced. Bin sizes per fold: HD ~1,815, MD ~548, ND ~147 miRNAs; each classifier used the top 50.
## Why this does NOT support the abstract's lung-cancer claim (controls, prereg-fixed wording)
- D6 random-feature baseline: 50 miRNAs picked AT RANDOM (10 draws) give mean AUROC 0.992 (accuracy 0.960). MD's 0.9999 beats that by 0.008, below the preregistered 0.05 margin. So "medium-dysregulation miRNAs carry useful information" is not distinguishable from "almost any 50 miRNAs separate these samples". Gate FAILED.
- D4 batch: two control-only groups (XA vs XB, no disease in either) are separated at AUROC 0.983 by the same RF; 5-way control cohort identity is 91.6% accurate vs 45.6% majority baseline. Large cohort/batch effects exist within controls. Cases (all prefix LK) come from a different cohort than every control: perfect confound of class and sample-title prefix, no within-cohort comparison is possible. D4(c) label signal AUROC is ~1.0 against each control group (range <= 0.001), which passes the "stable" gate only trivially (ceiling effect), so it does not rescue the claim.
- D5 age/sex: age + sex alone give AUROC 0.833 (LC 65.3 vs controls 50.8 years). Gate "confound material" is TRUE on that criterion. In a 900-pair sex- and age-matched subset (age 60.3 vs 60.0) the classifiers are unchanged (AUROC 0.9999), so age is not what drives the separation; the separation survives age matching but not the site confound.
- D3: selecting miRNAs on all data before CV instead of inside folds changes accuracy by <= 0.001: selection leakage is not the problem. D10: permuted-label control AUROC 0.51 (sane); 5 miRNAs alone give MD AUROC 0.994, 10 give 0.999.
## One result that cuts against a pure site artifact (D9, descriptive)
The 180 post-operative LC samples are from the same LK cohort as the pre-op cases. Models trained on pre-op vs controls call 0% (HD) and 0.6% (MD) of them cancer, with mean cancer probability 0.20 (HD) and 0.29 (MD); the pre-op samples they were trained on are 100% called cancer. If the models only learned the LK site or batch, post-op LK samples would score as cancer. They do not. That is consistent with a tumor-related serum change that goes away after surgery, but it cannot be separated here from "post-op samples were collected/processed differently", and the same patients' pre-op samples were in training, so this is not a held-out test.
## Net claim
D2 reproduces the abstract's pattern on an independent cohort, but because class is perfectly confounded with cohort (and controls show strong cohort effects), and random 50-miRNA sets do as well as MD (D6), this analysis does NOT show that medium-dysregulation miRNAs carry lung-cancer information beyond any miRNAs. Pre-registered wording applies: "classification of a confounded cohort contrast, not shown to be lung cancer biology". The D9 post-op result is the one piece of evidence pointing toward real signal; it needs a cohort with cases and controls collected together.
## D7 threshold sensitivity (descriptive)
MD accuracy 0.994 (edges 0.5/0.9/1.1/2.0), 0.991 (0.6/0.95/1.05/1.67), 0.983 (0.7/0.9/1.1/1.43); AUROC 0.9999 / 0.9997 / 0.9992. All near ceiling, so the bin definitions hardly matter.
## D8 sequence clustering (descriptive, pool selected on all data)
Mapped 2,511 of 2,565 array miRNAs to sequences (RNAcentral miRBase export); the HD+MD pool had 96 mapped miRNAs (of 100). K-means knee at k = 18. The student's n = 31 is NOT reproduced (gate needs 28-34). Random pools of 100 mapped miRNAs give knees 11-20 (5-95 percentile 11-19); the real knee is inside that range, so the clustering is not distinguishable from random miRNA pools. Sequence features: length, GC, 1- and 2-mer frequencies, seed 1-mers.
## Limits
One public serum cohort with a class/site confound; no external validation cohort; fold-change computed assuming log2-scale values per GEO processing text; ND is defined without a significance requirement; control group sizes uneven (XA 979, XB 992, PR 97, SA 80, BC 30; matrix has 2,178 controls where the series text says 1,774); D8 pool and D9 are descriptive; mapped-sequence coverage 96%; RNAcentral's miRBase release may differ from the array's (miRBase v21 era). The student's own dataset is not available, so this tests the hypothesis, not their numbers.
